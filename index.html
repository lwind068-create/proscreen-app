FAQ

Common questions

Do I need a credit card to start the free trial?
Yes, we collect your card upfront but you won't be charged for 7 days. You can cancel any time before the trial ends and you'll owe nothing. This lets us give you full access to every feature from day one without any manual steps.
Where does the stock data come from?
Live prices come from Polygon.io. Fundamentals (P/E, EPS, margins, ROE) come from Financial Modeling Prep. News from NewsAPI. AI analysis is powered by Anthropic's Claude model.
Is this compliant for use with clients?
ProScreen is a research and workflow tool, not an investment advisor. All AI analysis includes a disclaimer that it's for informational purposes only. You remain the licensed advisor responsible for all recommendations made to clients.
How is this different from Bloomberg Terminal?
Bloomberg costs $24,000/year and is built for institutional traders. ProScreen is built specifically for independent RIAs — simpler, AI-first, and focused on the client workflow. At $49/month it's accessible without a firm budget.
Can I cancel anytime?
Yes — no contracts, no cancellation fees. Cancel any time from your account settings.
What is the founding advisor rate?
The first 5 advisors who subscribe lock in $49/month forever — even when prices increase. You also get a 1-on-1 onboarding call and direct access to request features. Book a demo to claim your spot.
